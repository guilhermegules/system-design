These topics cover the essential principles for designing scalable, reliable, and distributed systems.

• **Scalability**: Understanding how to handle increased load, often using techniques like fan-out on write/read and caching (Example: Twitter Feed needs to serve millions of users and handle dramatic traffic spikes instantly).
• **Load Balancing**: Essential for distributing requests across services.
• **Caching**: Crucial for low latency and high availability. Study specific patterns and resilience strategies.
• **Sharding/Partitioning**: Distributing data across multiple servers for horizontal scaling.
• **CAP Theorem**: Understanding the trade-offs between Consistency and Availability. For instance, the Amazon Shopping Cart often prioritizes availability over strict consistency using eventual consistency.
